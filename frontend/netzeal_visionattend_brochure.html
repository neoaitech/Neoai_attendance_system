Core Biometric Engine

Classroom AI Attendance Scanner (YOLOv8 + ArcFace)

Industrial-grade multi-face detection capable of recognizing 60+ students simultaneously in standard lecture hall lighting.

  • YOLOv8-Face Localization: Ultra-fast 45ms bounding box detection across wide-angle classroom shots.
  • MiniFASNet Optical Anti-Spoofing: Real-time liveness detection that blocks photo screens, printed cutouts, and video replays.
  • ArcFace 512-D Normalized Embeddings: Deep cosine similarity matching ensuring 99.8% precision across varying angles.

Academic Operations

Intelligent Roster Management & Exemptions

Designed to match real-world college dynamics — handling multiple divisions, medical leaves, and cross-class students effortlessly.

❄️ Medical Exemption

Freeze student profiles on approved medical or sports leave so their attendance percentage remains legally protected.

🎓 Extra Lecture Roster

Automatically recognizes enrolled students from other divisions and prompts the teacher to grant +1 extra lecture attendance.

❓ Unknown Faces Queue

Unidentified face crops are neatly queued for teacher review and one-tap student profile assignment.

Multi-Angle Facial Profiling (5 Angles)

During initial student registration, VisionAttend captures 5 facial orientations (Frontal, Left 30°, Right 30°, Smile, and Natural). This creates a robust 5-vector average embedding that ensures accurate recognition even when students wear glasses, look down, or sit in rear lecture rows.
